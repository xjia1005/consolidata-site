One page for purchasing

Supply, contract and comparison

What you care about

  • Supply continuity and lead times
  • Contract terms and pricing structure
  • How this compares with the current supplier

What we prepared

  • Supply and lead-time summary
  • Contract term outline
  • Factual comparison table (no superiority claims)
[RA] No comparative superiority language. Only factual terms, prices and lead times with a stated source and date.
One page for finance / accounting

Budget impact at a glance

What you care about

  • What this costs per case and per year
  • Where the offset comes from
  • What assumptions the numbers rest on

What we prepared

  • One-page budget impact model
  • Volume × price × length-of-stay assumptions
  • Sensitivity: what changes the answer
[RA] Every cost figure cites its source and assumption. No guaranteed savings. The model is a planning tool, not a promise.
