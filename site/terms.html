Website and purchase terms

Please review the initial assessment's scope and delivery before you pay.

1. Your Growth Decision Assessment purchase

US$99 is a one-time charge, plus applicable tax shown by Stripe before payment. There is no subscription. The initial assessment report preserves your 30 answers and optional notes, compares plausible explanations for the areas you select, reviews four customer-value questions and proposes one bounded evidence check. The complete report is delivered only as a portable document format (PDF) attachment to your required delivery email after payment verification. The website provides a limited preview and delivery status.

The report is prepared automatically using consistent assessment rules. Your selected symptoms and evidence categories determine which alternatives and checks are included. Written answers and optional notes are preserved as context. They are not independently verified or interpreted by an external artificial intelligence model. The assessment does not establish a unique growth constraint or predict a business result. Human advisory review, account audits, interviews, content production, tests and ongoing services require a separate written agreement. A further purchase is optional.

2. Delivery and keeping your report

Answer the questions and review a limited preview first. Enter and confirm your work email, then review a separate purchase summary with the price and report details before continuing to Stripe to pay. Saving the email creates no payment session, sends no email, and does not subscribe you to marketing. Checkout is enabled only when the payment and email-delivery service is configured. The server must verify the payment record before preparing your complete report. Returning from checkout is not proof of payment.

Unsubmitted answers remain in the open page. Choosing Continue to email sends the confirmed selections and optional notes to our server. After verified payment, the report is sent to your delivery email as a PDF attachment. The full report is not displayed or offered for download on the website. The status page distinguishes payment verification, email processing and acceptance by the email service; acceptance does not guarantee arrival in the inbox. Please check your email spelling and spam folder, and contact support if delivery fails.

3. Access problems and refund requests

If you cannot access the questionnaire or generate your report, or want to request a refund, email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase email/reference and a description of the issue. Do not email card numbers. We can help check payment and report delivery, correct an address where appropriate, or arrange a resend. Refund requests are reviewed individually; this notice does not limit any mandatory rights that apply to your purchase.

4. Your information and decisions

Use accurate business-level answers. Leave out confidential customer records, payment details, passwords, medical information and unrelated sensitive data. Your report reflects self-reported answers. Unknowns remain unknown. You retain responsibility for your budgets, product claims, legal obligations and commercial decisions. Reach, orders, revenue, profit, campaign performance and a winning channel are not guaranteed.

5. Optional further services

Additional work begins only after a written agreement identifies the contracting parties, scope, responsibilities, permitted data, approvals, pricing, currency, taxes, delivery, cancellation and ownership. Your team retains customer relationships, account control and final approval of material and spending. Attribution alone does not establish additional sales caused by marketing.

6. Contact requests

You may contact us for report support or to discuss a separately scoped service. Sending a message does not book a meeting or start an engagement. A meeting or additional work is confirmed separately.

7. Payment and contact

CONSOLIDATA is our public brand. The Stripe checkout and your payment record identify the merchant processing your purchase. Questions about this website, delivery or purchase may be sent to alvinj@consolidatats.com.
