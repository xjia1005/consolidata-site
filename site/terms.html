Website and purchase terms

Please review the checkup's scope and delivery before you pay.

1. Your Marketing Checkup purchase

US$47 is a one-time charge, plus applicable tax shown by Stripe before payment. There is no subscription. You receive a ten-question browser-based self-assessment and an answer-based report covering described strengths, unknowns, up to three priorities, practical next actions, discussion prompts and a complete answer summary. Text, JSON and browser print/save-as-PDF options are included.

Fixed rules use your selected answers. Optional notes appear as written and do not change findings. The checkup does not connect to your advertising accounts, independently verify your records, use an external artificial intelligence model or predict sales. Human account review, interviews, content production, advertising tests and ongoing services are excluded and require a separate written agreement. A call or further purchase is optional.

2. Delivery and keeping your report

Payment is handled on Stripe. Continue to the questionnaire after successful payment. The questionnaire link is shareable; this edition has no account, per-order access authentication or cloud report storage. Visiting the questionnaire is not proof of payment.

Answers remain in the open browser page. Review and edit them, generate the report, then download or print it before refreshing or closing. Browser form recovery depends on your browser's settings. We cannot recover unsaved answers or reports. You may run the questionnaire again.

3. Access problems and refund requests

If you cannot access the questionnaire or generate your report, or want to request a refund, email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase email/reference and a description of the issue. Do not email card numbers. We can provide the questionnaire link and help troubleshoot. Refund requests are reviewed individually; this notice does not limit any mandatory rights that apply to your purchase.

4. Your information and decisions

Use accurate business-level answers. Leave out confidential customer records, payment details, passwords, medical information and unrelated sensitive data. Your report reflects self-reported answers. Unknowns remain unknown. You retain responsibility for your budgets, product claims, legal obligations and commercial decisions. Reach, orders, revenue, profit, campaign performance and a winning channel are not guaranteed.

5. Optional further services

Additional work begins only after a written agreement identifies the contracting parties, scope, responsibilities, permitted data, approvals, pricing, currency, taxes, delivery, cancellation and ownership. Your team retains customer relationships, account control and final approval of material and spending. Attribution alone does not establish additional sales caused by marketing.

6. Contact requests

The homepage prepares a draft in your browser. You choose whether to copy or open it in your email app and send it. Preparing the draft sends and saves nothing on our server. A meeting time is confirmed personally by email; no calendar slot is booked by the website.

7. Payment and contact

CONSOLIDATA is our public brand. The Stripe checkout and your payment record identify the merchant processing your purchase. Questions about this website, delivery or purchase may be sent to alvinj@consolidatats.com.
