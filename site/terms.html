Website and service terms

A useful sales review needs clear responsibilities, permitted data and agreed outcomes. These boundaries describe the proposed service; paid work begins after a written agreement identifies the contracting parties, scope and commercial terms.

1. Agreed scope

The written proposal selects one product or service, one buyer, one starting channel and one next action. It defines the buying-reason review, content or sales material, inquiry route, sales follow-up and quote records, outcome review, deliverables, revisions, timing and fees. Interviews, filming, advertising, additional channels, websites and integrations are included only when agreed.

2. Customer responsibilities

The customer provides accurate product information, materials it has permission to use, a designated reviewer, timely approvals and agreed sales and cost records. The customer retains its goals, relationships, pricing, budgets, product claims, publication approvals and final decisions, and is responsible for fulfillment, support and its legal obligations.

3. Review, publication and communication

Material is prepared for customer review. Product, performance, safety, legal and brand statements require the customer's appropriate reviewer. Publishing, sending messages and account actions take place only within documented approval and permissions. The written scope assigns responsibility for responding to inquiries, following up and issuing quotes. Unapproved drafts must not be treated as public material.

4. Evidence and commercial outcomes

Owner descriptions and generated scenarios remain hypotheses until supported. Reviews distinguish attention, qualified inquiries, opportunities, quotes, wins, losses, unresolved outcomes and collected revenue. Contribution calculations identify the revenue and costs included; missing data remains unknown. Attributed sales do not by themselves establish additional sales caused by the work. Time savings do not by themselves establish profit.

5. Results and decisions

The agreement defines the work to be delivered. CONSOLIDATA does not guarantee reach, rankings, conversion gains, orders, revenue, profit or a winning channel. Product, price, sales response, buyer access, inventory, platforms and market conditions affect results. The service supports commercial decisions; it does not replace qualified engineering, safety, legal, medical, tax or financial judgment.

6. Access, tools and consent

Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Permitted data, exports, account permissions, tools, confidentiality, storage, retention and deletion are agreed before use. Customer information may be used with artificial intelligence tools only within the approved purpose and controls. Interviews or recordings require agreed scope and appropriate participant consent.

7. Fees, timing and cancellation

Scope and pricing are set in a written proposal after discussion. It specifies currency, taxes, billing, start dates, delivery and acceptance criteria, cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms before payment. Third-party costs and advertising budgets require prior approval. There is no live checkout on this website.

8. Ownership and public use

The agreement identifies ownership and permitted use of customer materials, methods, templates, working files, evidence and final deliverables. Public use of customer names, quotes, images, recordings, testimonials or results requires documented permission.

9. Call requests

The homepage prepares a call-request draft locally in your browser. You choose whether to copy it or open your email app and send it. The website does not automatically send or save form entries, take payment or book a calendar slot. A request is an invitation to discuss the work; the meeting time is confirmed by email.

10. Contact and contracting details

Questions may be sent to alvinj@consolidatats.com. CONSOLIDATA is the public brand. The written agreement identifies the legal contracting party, business address, governing law and any additional terms before paid work begins.
