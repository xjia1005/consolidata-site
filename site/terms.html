Terms, scope
and expectations.

Review the assessment purchase and the separately agreed enterprise services before committing.

1. Your Growth Clarity Assessment purchase

US$99 is a one-time charge, plus applicable tax shown by Stripe before payment. There is no subscription. The initial assessment uses all 30 answers and optional notes to clarify who you serve, the buying situation, problem, desired benefit, alternatives and buying-reason hypothesis; examine your current growth decision; propose ranked explanations when the information supports a comparison, preserve credible alternatives and source-linked quotations, identify contradictions and unknowns, review four customer-value questions and propose one bounded next check. Your complete answer record is included. The complete report is delivered as a portable document format (PDF) attachment to your required delivery email after payment verification. The website provides a limited preview and delivery status.

The report is an artificial intelligence assisted initial analysis prepared using the configured OpenAI or Anthropic service after verified payment. Your delivery email and payment details are excluded from the analysis input; OpenAI requests use store: false as explained in the privacy notice. Your answers and business records are not independently verified. A source-linked quotation identifies what you submitted; it does not prove an explanation. Insufficient or contradictory information may produce an evidence-gathering plan with no ranked explanation. The proposed next check describes an observable result, how it could change the decision, a proposed owner, resource needs and a bounded next step. You review and agree any action or resource commitment separately.

The report does not establish a proven root cause or guarantee growth. It explains when separately scoped ConsoliData research, positioning, offer, content, outreach or measurement work could help, and when a product, continued-use, operations or financial issue needs another responsible owner. Human advisory review, account audits, interviews, content production, tests and ongoing services require a separate written agreement. A further purchase is optional.

2. Delivery and keeping your report

Answer the questions and review a limited preview first. Enter and confirm your work email, then review a separate purchase summary with the price and report details before continuing to Stripe to pay. Saving the email creates no payment session, sends no email, and does not subscribe you to marketing. Checkout is enabled only when the payment, analysis and email-delivery services meet the configured readiness rules. The server must verify the payment record before preparing your complete report. Returning from checkout is not proof of payment.

Unsubmitted answers remain in the open page. Choosing Continue to email sends the confirmed selections and optional notes to our server. After verified payment, the report is sent to your delivery email as a PDF attachment. The full report is not displayed or offered for download on the website. The status page distinguishes payment verification, email processing and acceptance by the email service; acceptance does not guarantee arrival in the inbox. Please check your email spelling and spam folder, and contact support if delivery fails.

3. Access problems and refund requests

If you cannot access the questionnaire or generate your report, or want to request a refund, email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase email/reference and a description of the issue. Do not email card numbers. We can help check payment and report delivery, correct an address where appropriate, or arrange a resend. Refund requests are reviewed individually; this notice does not limit any mandatory rights that apply to your purchase.

4. Your information and decisions

Use accurate business-level answers. Leave out confidential customer records, payment details, passwords, medical information and unrelated sensitive data. Your report reflects self-reported answers. Unknowns remain unknown. You retain responsibility for your budgets, product claims, legal obligations and commercial decisions. Reach, orders, revenue, profit, campaign performance and a winning channel are not guaranteed.

5. Optional enterprise services and acceptance

Enterprise services are scoped and quoted for the agreed work. See service details. The exact engagement scope, fee, applicable taxes and approved third-party costs are agreed in writing before work. Existing signed agreements retain their agreed commercial terms. Research recruitment, relevant customer groups, evidence sufficiency, comparison design and any extensions are agreed for the actual decision. No fixed sample size alone establishes sufficient evidence.

For the first enterprise validation there is no prepaid service fee. Before work begins, agree the scope, required inputs, period, acceptance standard, evidence, amount due on acceptance and payment date. Payment follows agreed acceptance. If the valid first validation misses that standard, no service fee is charged for that validation. Insufficient data, unmet client prerequisites and additional research or retests follow separately agreed terms; continued work is not unlimited. Later monthly billing and renewal are expressly agreed. The independently purchased US$99 assessment has separate paid-product terms.

Research delivery, a useful test decision and an acquisition or sales result are different outcomes. If the agreed acceptance condition is qualified opportunities or sales, completing a report cannot substitute for that condition. Advertising spending, participant incentives and other purchases have a separate approved payer, cap and terms. Included research or advertising management is never billed twice.

Additional work begins only after a written agreement identifies the contracting parties, scope, responsibilities, permitted data, approvals, pricing, currency, taxes, delivery, cancellation and ownership. Your team retains customer relationships, account control and final approval of material and spending. Attribution alone does not establish additional sales caused by marketing.

6. Contact requests

You may contact us for report support or to discuss a separately scoped service. Sending a message does not book a meeting or start an engagement. Use the marketing-discussion booking page to open the existing appointment scheduler, choose a time and complete its confirmation. Opening a page alone does not book a meeting. Additional work requires a separate agreement.

The dedicated local sandbox page is for synthetic scenarios and simulated US$99 payments only. No actual money is charged and no report email is sent. After verified simulated payment, the report document is retained for private operator review. Sandbox completion does not establish a customer purchase or a commercial result.

Earlier orders keep the scope, price and delivery terms agreed at purchase. This new assessment and its price do not require an earlier buyer to purchase again. See the earlier assessment terms and contact support with the original purchase reference if you need help.
