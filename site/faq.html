BEFORE YOU START

A useful report. A clear next step.

Who is the checkup for?

Owners and authorized decision-makers of already-selling businesses in the United States and Canada. It is most useful when you already spend on marketing and need to review one offer, buyer situation or budget decision.

What does the assessment include?

Ten questions with optional notes and a limited preview. You can then review an optional report purchase: described strengths, unknowns, up to three priorities, practical next actions, team discussion prompts and your full answer summary. After verified payment, the full report is sent only as a PDF attachment to your delivery email. The report price appears in the purchase review, with any applicable tax shown on the secure payment page before you pay. There is no subscription or advertising budget included.

Is this an AI analysis of my advertising accounts?

The report uses fixed rules and your selected answers on our server. Notes are included as written and do not change findings. No advertising accounts are connected and no external artificial intelligence model processes your answers. Account audits, interviews, campaign testing and human review require a separate scope.

When do I pay?

Start the assessment, answer ten questions and review your answers. Enter and confirm your work email, then review the report price and details before secure payment. Starting and saving your email do not charge you or subscribe you to marketing emails. If the payment or email-delivery service is not ready, you can still save your email; final payment stays disabled with an explanation.

How do I receive my report?

Your complete report is sent as a PDF email attachment after payment is verified. The website shows only a limited preview and purchase/delivery status. An opaque reference in browser session storage helps restore status. If the email is missing, check your spam folder or contact us with your purchase reference. Email-service acceptance does not guarantee inbox arrival; support can review delivery problems.

Does it guarantee which campaign will sell more?

The report helps clarify what your answers support and what to check next. Campaign decisions need actual records, costs and an appropriate comparison. Additional sales, revenue, profit and a winning channel are not guaranteed.

Do I have to book a call or buy another service?

Your report stands on its own. A 20-minute discussion is optional. The website prepares an email draft for you to review and send; a time is confirmed personally. Follow-on deliverables, responsibilities, approvals and fees are agreed in writing before work starts.

What if I cannot access or generate the report?

Email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase reference and the problem. Never email card details. We can help with payment, address correction where appropriate and delivery; see the purchase and refund-request details.
