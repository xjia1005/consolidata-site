QUESTIONS BEFORE WORK

Know which sales action deserves your next effort.

Buying reasons, content, inquiries and sales results often sit in separate places. We connect the agreed records around one product, one buyer, one starting channel and one next action.

Who is this for?

Business owners, chief executive officers (CEOs) and commercial decision-makers with an existing product or service, a defined buyer and a purchase or quote path. Your team needs someone who can approve material, respond to inquiries and share the outcome records agreed for the work.

What happens in the 20-minute sales-workflow call?

Bring one product or service page and the sales question you need to resolve. We discuss who buys, why they buy, the current channel, how inquiries receive follow-up, and which quote and outcome records are available. If there is a useful fit, the next step is a written proposal with scope, responsibilities, timing and fees.

What does the service deliver?

The agreed scope connects buying-reason evidence to review-ready content or sales material, a supported inquiry route, sales follow-up and quote records, and a review of won, lost and unresolved opportunities. Where permitted data is available, it also follows collected revenue and known costs so the next action has a commercial basis.

How is artificial intelligence used?

Artificial intelligence (AI) can help organize approved evidence, draft material and review patterns in the agreed records. Your designated reviewers check the work before use. Tool use, customer-data permissions and access are agreed in advance; your company retains product claims, relationships, approvals and business decisions.

Do we need customer interviews or a new company knowledge base?

Your existing product information, sales records and permitted customer evidence are the starting point. Owner explanations and generated scenarios remain hypotheses until supported. Interviews, recruiting or additional research are scoped when they answer a specific gap, with appropriate consent.

Do we have to use Instagram or appear on camera?

We agree one starting channel around your buyer and current sales path. The proposal defines the content formats, source material and people needed. Founder appearances, filming and additional channels are included only when agreed.

Who handles inquiries, follow-up and quotes?

The proposal assigns an owner for inquiry response, follow-up and quotes, including what information is recorded and when outcomes are reviewed. Your company controls customer relationships, pricing, commercial promises and final sales decisions. Any sending, publishing or account access requires documented permission.

How do you tell whether the work helped sales?

We keep attention, qualified inquiries, opportunities, quotes, wins, losses and collected revenue as separate stages. Agreed records include unsuccessful attempts and unknown outcomes. Contribution means collected revenue less the costs included in the agreed calculation; missing cost data stays unknown. An attributed sale alone does not prove that the work caused an additional sale, and time saved alone does not establish profit.

Do you guarantee more sales or a winning channel?

We commit to the deliverables and review process in the written agreement. Sales also depend on the product, price, buyer access, inventory, follow-up and market conditions. We do not guarantee reach, conversion gains, orders, revenue, profit or a winning channel. Small or incomplete samples may leave the result uncertain.

Who approves and publishes the material?

Your designated reviewer approves product details, claims, offers and brand statements before use. Publication happens only within the permissions and schedule you approve. Unsupported statements are held for clarification, and draft material remains subject to review.

How are scope, fees and timing agreed?

After discussion, the written proposal defines deliverables, revisions, responsibilities, measurements, timing, fees, payment dates and cancellation terms. Advertising spend, filming, additional channels, recruiting, websites and integrations require explicit agreement. This website has no checkout.

Do you need passwords or private customer files?

Do not put passwords, payment information or sensitive customer files in the call request. We agree permitted exports or limited account permissions, the tools used, confidentiality and retention before processing customer information.

Does preparing the call request send it or book a meeting?

The request builder prepares a draft in your browser. You can review and copy it, or choose to open it in your email app and send it yourself. The website does not automatically send or save form entries, and it does not book a calendar slot. The meeting time is confirmed by email.
