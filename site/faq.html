Who is the checkup for?

Owners, chief executive officers and authorized decision-makers of already-selling consumer businesses in the United States. It is most useful when you already spend on marketing and need to review one offer, buyer situation or budget decision.

What do I get for US$27?

Ten questions with optional notes and a complete report: described strengths, unknowns, up to three priorities, practical next actions, team discussion prompts and your full answer summary. You can edit answers and download text or JSON, or print/save as PDF. Applicable tax is shown by Stripe before payment. There is no subscription or advertising budget included.

Is this an AI analysis of my advertising accounts?

The report uses fixed rules and your selected answers in your browser. Notes are included as written and do not change findings. No advertising accounts are connected and no external artificial intelligence model processes your answers. Account audits, interviews, campaign testing and human review require a separate scope.

How do I pay and open the questionnaire?

Choose Get the checkup to open the product details and payment form together. Use an available payment method. After successful payment, continue to the questionnaire. Link and wallet availability depend on device and eligibility. If you lose the return page, email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase reference for the link.

Can I come back to my report?

Download or print it before refreshing or closing. This edition has no account or cloud report storage. Answers stay in the open page, subject to your browser's form recovery. We cannot recover unsaved answers. The questionnaire link is shareable and not bound to individual orders; you can answer again.

Does it guarantee which campaign will sell more?

The report helps clarify what your answers support and what to check next. Campaign decisions need actual records, costs and an appropriate comparison. Additional sales, revenue, profit and a winning channel are not guaranteed.

Do I have to book a call or buy another service?

Your report stands on its own. A 20-minute discussion is optional. The website prepares an email draft for you to review and send; a time is confirmed personally. Follow-on deliverables, responsibilities, approvals and fees are agreed in writing before work starts.
