BEFORE YOU START

A growth decision. A useful next check.

Who is the assessment for?

Our current focus is startup leaders in the United States and Canada whose companies received at least one venture-funding round in the past three years. They have users or customers, believe the product meets a real need and face growth below expectations. A company owner, chief executive, product lead or marketing leader should bring one current decision, relevant business information and the ability to act on a next check. Funding and willingness to pay do not establish the cause of a growth problem.

What does US$99 include?

US$99 is a one-time report purchase, plus applicable tax shown before payment. The initial report preserves your 30 answers and optional notes, compares possible explanations for the areas you select, reviews four customer-value questions and proposes one bounded evidence check. The complete report is sent only as an email attachment after verified payment. There is no subscription, advertising budget or campaign execution included.

Why are there 30 questions?

Six starting questions define the decision, customer situation, reported gap and available evidence. Six groups of four examine reach, customer response, the first useful result, purchase, continued use and missing evidence. Unknown or deferred answers remain visible. Each group supports a narrower investigation; the questionnaire alone does not certify a single root cause.

What are the four customer-value questions?

What result matters to the customer? What makes that result credible? How long must the customer wait for useful progress? What effort, cost or disruption is involved? These questions guide the relevant customer experience. Actual delivery, costs and business results still require evidence.

Who prepares the report?

The report is prepared automatically using consistent assessment rules. Your selected symptoms and evidence categories determine which alternatives and checks are included. Your written answers and notes are preserved as context; they are not independently verified or interpreted by an external artificial intelligence model. Human advisory review, account audits, customer interviews and tests require a separate agreement.

When do I pay?

Start answering, review the 30 answers and confirm your work email first. Then review the US$99 purchase summary and applicable tax before choosing secure payment. Starting and saving your email do not charge you or subscribe you to marketing. When the report purchase is unavailable, payment remains disabled with an explanation.

How do I receive and keep the report?

The complete report is sent as a portable document format (PDF) attachment to your saved email after payment verification. The website shows a limited preview and purchase or delivery status. Keep the email and attachment. Email-service acceptance differs from arrival in your inbox; check the address and spam folder, and contact support with your purchase reference if it is missing. The website does not display the full report or offer a full-report download.

Will this identify the single factor that limits growth?

The report helps you describe the situation, keep plausible explanations in view and plan a check that could distinguish between them. Confirming a constraint requires relevant evidence, and several factors may interact. Marketing, product, sales, onboarding or delivery work may be appropriate. Sales, profit and growth improvements are not guaranteed.

Do I need to buy further services?

The report is a standalone initial assessment. Any additional records review, interviews, positioning, service-package design, content work or marketing execution requires a separate written scope and commercial agreement. No further service or meeting is booked automatically.

What if I have a delivery problem or want to request a refund?

Email alvinj@consolidatats.com with your purchase reference and the issue. Never send card details. We can review payment and report delivery, correct an address where appropriate or arrange a resend. See the purchase and refund-request details.
