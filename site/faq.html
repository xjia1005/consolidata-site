What the assessment includes, how the report is prepared and how further services work.

Who is the assessment for?

The assessment was designed around a company with a product, customers and a current growth decision. It may be useful to a team seeking to organize its existing information; its questions and limitations can be reviewed before purchase. Funding is not required.

What does US$99 include?

US$99 is a one-time report purchase, plus applicable tax shown before payment. Your report helps organize who you serve, their buying situation, problem, desired benefit, alternatives and a candidate buying reason. It also examines your growth observations, plausible explanations, missing evidence and one proposed next check, with the complete answer record. The full report follows verified payment at your delivery email as an attachment. Further services are optional.

Why are there 30 questions?

The questions bring the buying context and growth observations together. They cover your decision, customer and offer, how suitable customers encounter and understand it, first useful use, purchase, continued use and evidence. Each group helps narrow what to examine next. The purpose is a useful decision brief, not a score based on how many questions you complete. Unknown or deferred answers remain visible.

Can the report identify the single thing holding us back?

It can organize a bounded set of plausible explanations and propose a check that might distinguish them. Answers alone do not establish a unique cause. Several factors may interact, and sparse or conflicting information can require evidence gathering first. Product, continued-use, operations or financial issues may need another responsible owner. Sales, profit and growth are not guaranteed.

Who prepares the report?

After server-verified payment, the configured OpenAI or Anthropic service interprets all 30 answers and optional notes to prepare an artificial intelligence assisted initial analysis. The provider is named on the assessment page before purchase. Your delivery email and payment details are excluded from the analysis input. The service copies cited quotations directly from your saved answers and notes; they establish text provenance, not truth or a causal diagnosis. The report contains provisional explanations, credible alternatives, contradictions and unknowns. Human advisory review, interviews, account audits and execution are separately agreed. See our privacy notice.

What are the four customer-value questions?

What result matters to this customer? What makes achieving it credible? How long must the customer wait? What effort, cost or disruption is involved? These questions help examine the offer and experience. Delivery capability and actual customer outcomes still require evidence.

When do I pay?

Review the 30 answers before payment. If everything is unknown or deferred and there are no substantive notes, add one concrete product, customer or decision fact before checkout. Other answers may remain unknown. The server must confirm purchase eligibility. Confirm your work email, review US$99 and applicable tax, then choose secure payment when available. Saving your email does not send a message, create a payment session or subscribe you to marketing. If services are unavailable, payment stays disabled with an explanation.

How do I receive and share the report?

After payment verification, the complete report is sent as a portable document format (PDF) email attachment. Keep the email and share the attachment with colleagues as appropriate. The site shows a limited preview and payment/delivery status; it has no public personal-report download. Email-service acceptance differs from inbox arrival. Check your address and spam folder and contact support if delivery is missing.

What happens after the report?

Use it with your team, or book a 20-minute conversation about the report and possible next work. Choose an available time in the existing Google appointment scheduler and complete its confirmation. Opening the scheduler alone does not book a meeting or start a subscription or engagement. See services and pricing for scoped options.

How do you choose the research sample?

The research plan starts with the decision and the evidence needed. Relevant groups can include successful, struggling, departed and non-buying customers. We agree recruitment, coverage, counterevidence, a sufficiency review and any extension before work. A fixed interview count alone does not establish enough evidence. The scope, fee, participant costs and acceptance are agreed in writing for each engagement.

Can you compare channels and content formats?

Yes, within an agreed test matrix and budget. The scope and fee for a bounded first phase are agreed before work begins. Comparisons are staged around a main contrast, or use a justified structured design. Different channels may reach different audiences, so response differences do not automatically prove one channel caused better sales. Further waves, media spend and creative work are agreed explicitly.

How is the first enterprise validation paid?

The standalone US$99 report is a separate paid product. For the first enterprise validation, agree scope, inputs, period, evidence, acceptance standard, amount and payment date in writing before work. There is no prepaid service fee. Payment follows the agreed acceptance; if a valid first validation misses that standard, no service fee is charged for that validation. Insufficient data, unmet prerequisites and additional rounds follow separately agreed terms. Advertising and other third-party costs have a separate approved payer and cap. See the service terms.

See purchase terms.

Your earlier order keeps its original scope, price and delivery terms. The new assessment is a separate product; you do not need to pay the new price to recover an earlier purchase. See the earlier assessment terms and contact alvinj@consolidatats.com with your original purchase reference for help.
