Why deals stall

One deck. Four people. Zero fit.

To get into a hospital, four different people have to say yes — and each one is judging something different.

Surgeon
Wants clinical evidence and a technique that fits.
OR Nurse
Wants a clear setup, training, and reprocessing.
Purchasing
Wants supply, lead time, and a clean contract.
Finance
Wants one number: what it costs, what it saves.

Send the same deck to all four, and none of them reads it past page two. The deal stalls on material, not on the product.
