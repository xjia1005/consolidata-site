Privacy notice

The Marketing Checkup and contact-request builder run in your browser. Stripe handles payment on its own hosted page.

1. Your answers and report

Selections and optional notes stay in the open page's memory. The checkup does not send them to CONSOLIDATA, Stripe or an artificial intelligence provider, or store them in cookies, local storage or session storage. Fixed rules organize your selected answers; notes appear as written and do not change findings. No external language model processes your answers.

Refresh or close the page and the checkup resets, subject to your browser's own form recovery. Download or print a copy before leaving. Files you save or share are under your control. There is no account, online report storage or automatic report email. The questionnaire URL is shareable and is not tied to an individual order. Do not enter personal customer details, passwords, confidential records or sensitive files.

2. Stripe payment

Choosing checkout opens Stripe. Stripe processes payment, payment methods, purchase email and any required tax information under Stripe's privacy policy. We can access the merchant purchase record in Stripe for payment administration, support and applicable recordkeeping. Assessment answers are not attached to the checkout link. Returning to the questionnaire does not verify an order. Payment does not automatically enroll you in marketing emails.

3. Contact requests and email

The request builder uses your entries in page memory to prepare text. Copying puts the text on your clipboard; opening an email draft passes it to your email app. You choose whether to send. Email providers process messages you send, and we use the information to respond, provide support or arrange the next step. No meeting is booked automatically. Phone or WeChat details are optional and used for the agreed contact purpose.

4. Technical information

Our hosting may record standard request information such as internet protocol address, time, path, referrer and browser information for security, reliability and troubleshooting. This is separate from assessment answers and request fields, which this site's code does not transmit. Our site adds no analytics service, advertising pixel or cookies. Stripe's separate page has its own controls and policies.

5. Further services and requests

Any processing of customer files, interviews, account exports or confidential information for additional services requires an agreed scope and controls. For questions about information you sent us or a request concerning it, email alvinj@consolidatats.com. We may need enough information to locate and verify your request. Records required for legal or payment obligations may need to be retained.
