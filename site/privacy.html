Privacy notice

This notice describes the intended minimum-data approach. It must be updated with the final business identity, service providers, retention schedule, jurisdiction, and contact before publication.

1. Information collected

The fit-review intake is intended to collect a name, work email, company, public product-page address, and a short description of the buyer decision. This local preview does not transmit or store form entries.

2. Engagement information

After a signed engagement, ConsoliData may process approved product materials, participant contact details, interview recordings, transcripts, consent records, research notes, review comments, content drafts, and agreed aggregate commercial events.

3. Information we do not need

Do not send account passwords, payment-card data, medical information, government identifiers, confidential customer lists, or unrelated sensitive files through the public intake.

4. How information is used

Information is used to assess fit, respond to the request, deliver the signed scope, preserve the evidence trail, obtain approvals, measure agreed events, invoice the customer, and meet legal or security obligations.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with service providers needed for communication, scheduling, recording, transcription, storage, analytics, payment, or delivery. The final notice will identify provider categories and applicable cross-border processing.

6. Artificial intelligence tools

No confidential interview or customer data will be submitted to an artificial intelligence service unless the signed agreement identifies the permitted tool, purpose, access controls, retention behavior, and customer approval.

7. Retention

The engagement agreement will define retention for original recordings, transcripts, consent records, deliverables, billing records, and fit-review requests. Data should be kept only as long as needed for the agreed purpose, legal obligations, and evidence integrity.

8. Security

Access should be limited to people and services needed for the engagement. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Security incidents and access requests will follow the final documented process.

9. Website analytics and cookies

This local preview uses no analytics, advertising pixels, or cookies. The notice must be revised before any analytics, advertising, form, scheduling, or payment service is added.

10. Contact

A monitored domain email and legal business identity must be inserted before publication. Privacy requests cannot be accepted through the inactive preview form.
