Privacy notice

You can prepare and review a sales-workflow call request before deciding whether to send it. The website keeps that preparation in your browser and has no form-submission backend, account, checkout, calendar booking, analytics service or advertising pixel.

1. Preparing a request in your browser

The request builder asks for your name, company, work email and sales question. Phone, WeChat, public product or service page, and preferred times and time zone are optional. The website code uses these values in the open page to prepare a draft; it does not transmit them to a server or save them in cookies, local storage or session storage. Your browser may manage autofill or form recovery under its own settings.

2. Copying or sending a request

Choosing to copy places the prepared text on your device's clipboard. Choosing the email-app option passes the draft to your email application. You review and send the message yourself; opening a draft does not send it. If you send it, your email provider and ours process the message. A call request does not book a meeting. The time is confirmed by email.

3. Email contact

We use the contact details and business information you choose to email to respond, assess fit and arrange the next step. Include only the information needed for that discussion. Phone or WeChat details, if supplied, are used for the contact purpose you agree.

4. Website technical information

Website hosting may record standard request information such as internet protocol address, time, requested path, referrer and browser information for security, reliability and troubleshooting. This is separate from the request builder, which does not submit its entries. The website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising pixels.

5. Information for agreed work

Before an engagement, the written scope defines permitted product materials, customer evidence, content drafts, review comments and commercial records. This may include inquiry and quote status, won and lost outcomes, collected revenue and relevant costs where available and authorized. Interviews, recordings, personal customer information, account permissions and confidential files require explicit agreement and appropriate consent.

6. Information to keep out of the call request

Do not enter or email passwords, payment-card data, medical information, government identifiers, confidential customer lists or unrelated sensitive files. An initial call request needs a business question and a way to respond.

7. Tools and service providers

Email providers process the messages you choose to send, and website hosting handles page requests. Any tools used for customer delivery, including artificial intelligence, transcription, storage, publishing or measurement, must be identified with their approved purpose, access and data-handling conditions before customer information is used.

8. Use, access and retention

Information is used to respond to requests and deliver the agreed scope, obtain approvals, review agreed outcomes and maintain required business records. Access is limited to the people and services needed for those purposes. The engagement agreement defines confidentiality, retention and deletion for customer information. Email correspondence is retained as needed for follow-up, business records, security and legal obligations.

9. Changes and questions

This notice must be reviewed before adding a form backend, scheduling, payment, analytics or other service that processes website entries. For privacy questions or correction requests, email alvinj@consolidatats.com. The written agreement identifies the legal contracting party and business address before paid work begins.
