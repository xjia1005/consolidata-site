Your information,
step by step.

Your answers, purchase and report delivery are handled in separate steps. Submit only information you have permission to share.

1. Your answers and report

While you answer and review the questions, selections and optional notes remain in the open page. When you confirm and choose Continue to email, the website sends them to our server and stores them with an opaque assessment reference. In the separate work-email step, we store your confirmed address with that assessment, even if you do not proceed to payment. Saving it does not send an email, create a payment session or subscribe you to marketing. If you later continue to payment, we associate the payment and delivery status with the same assessment. We use these records to prepare the report, deliver it, prevent duplicate fulfillment, and provide purchase support.

After verified payment, all 30 answers and optional notes are sent to the configured analysis provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to prepare an artificial intelligence assisted initial analysis. Your delivery email, payment details and opaque assessment reference are excluded from the analysis input. For OpenAI, OpenAI requests use store: false, asking OpenAI not to store the response for later retrieval; it does not promise zero provider retention. OpenAI processes submitted analysis input under its applicable service terms and privacy policy. We retain the submitted answer record and generated analysis on our server for report preparation and support. Anthropic requests use its Messages API and do not support the OpenAI store setting. Anthropic processes inputs under its applicable API terms and privacy policy. The configured provider is shown on the assessment page before purchase. Only submit information you have permission to share.

The report links quotations to your submitted answers and notes. These links establish the origin of the text, not its truth or a proven root cause. Provisional explanations, alternatives, contradictions, unknowns and proposed checks remain subject to review. The complete report is generated on our server and sent as a portable document format (PDF) email attachment through our email provider. The browser does not receive the full report.

The website stores an opaque assessment reference in browser session storage to restore purchase and delivery status after checkout. It does not store your answers, report or email in browser storage. Clearing session storage can remove this status shortcut; it does not delete submitted server records. Do not enter personal customer details, passwords, confidential records or sensitive files.

2. Stripe payment and report email

After you save your work email, review the purchase details and choose secure payment, Stripe processes payment, payment methods, email and any required billing or tax information under Stripe's privacy policy. The checkout uses an opaque assessment reference; your assessment answers and notes are not sent to Stripe. We use the merchant payment record and a verified payment notification to associate your purchase with your report. Returning from checkout does not verify an order.

Your delivery email is used for the report attachment, related purchase support and necessary delivery notices. Our email provider processes the message and attachment to send them. Payment and email-service acceptance are separate from actual inbox arrival. Purchasing does not enroll you in marketing emails. If your email needs correcting or a report has not arrived, contact us with your purchase reference.

3. Contact requests and email

Email providers process messages you choose to send us. We use the information to respond, provide report support or discuss an agreed next step. Contacting us does not automatically book a meeting or start further services.

Local sandbox review

The dedicated local sandbox assessment uses a separate service, local records and browser-session reference. It uses simulated Stripe payments; no actual money is charged and no report email is sent. A test reference email is stored for the sandbox record. After verified simulated payment, synthetic answers and notes may be sent to the configured analysis provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) under the analysis controls above, and the generated report document is retained for private operator review. Use synthetic business scenarios only.

Private Customer Survey

After booking, you may receive a private survey link. With your agreement, we store your answers in our AWS account for preparing your consultation. Anyone with your link can access your survey, so keep it confidential. You can skip questions. Do not include passwords, payment details or customer contact lists. To ask about your information, retention or deletion, contact alvinj@consolidatats.com.

Report-discussion booking

Our booking page links to a separate Google appointment scheduler. We do not automatically send your assessment answers, email, report or assessment reference through that link. If you choose to book, Google handles the information you enter under its privacy policy. Complete the scheduler confirmation to book a time. You choose separately whether to share your report with us for the discussion.

4. Technical information

Our hosting may record standard request information such as internet protocol address, time, path, referrer and browser information for security, reliability and troubleshooting. Confirmed assessment answers and your separately saved work email are submitted as described above. A non-personal source code may also be stored with an assessment to relate a purchase to its published content. Our site adds no analytics service, advertising pixel or cookies. Stripe's separate page has its own controls and policies.

5. Further services and requests

Any processing of customer files, interviews, account exports or confidential information for additional services requires an agreed scope and controls. For questions about information you sent us or a request concerning it, email alvinj@consolidatats.com. We may need enough information to locate and verify your request. Records required for legal or payment obligations may need to be retained.
