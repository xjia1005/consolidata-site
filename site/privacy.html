Privacy notice

This website uses a minimum-data approach. It has no web form, account, checkout, analytics service, advertising pixel, or cookie-based tracking.

1. Website information

The web server may record standard request information such as internet protocol address, timestamp, requested path, referrer, and browser user-agent for security, reliability, and troubleshooting. The website does not ask you to create an account or submit a web form.

2. Email contact

If you choose an email link, your message is sent through your email provider to ours. We use the name, work contact details, company, public product page, and buyer question you choose to send only to respond, assess fit, and arrange the next step.

3. Engagement information

After a signed engagement, ConsoliData may process approved product materials, participant contact details, interview recordings, transcripts, consent records, research notes, review comments, content drafts, and agreed aggregate commercial events under the signed scope.

4. Information we do not need

Do not email account passwords, payment-card data, medical information, government identifiers, confidential customer lists, or unrelated sensitive files.

5. How information is used

Information is used to respond, assess fit, deliver the signed scope, preserve the agreed evidence trail, obtain approvals, measure agreed events, invoice the customer, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

6. Sharing and service providers

Website hosting and email providers process the minimum technical and message data needed to operate those services. Any later scheduling, recording, transcription, storage, analytics, payment, or delivery provider is identified in the signed scope or an updated notice before it is used for customer information.

7. Artificial intelligence tools

No confidential interview or customer data will be submitted to an artificial intelligence service unless the signed agreement identifies the permitted tool, purpose, access controls, retention behavior, and customer approval.

8. Retention

Initial fit-call email is retained only as needed for follow-up, records, security, and legal obligations. A signed engagement defines retention for recordings, transcripts, consent records, deliverables, and billing records.

9. Security

Access is limited to people and services needed for communication or the signed engagement. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

10. Website analytics and cookies

This website currently uses no analytics, advertising pixels, or cookies. This notice will be updated before any analytics, form, scheduling, or payment service is added.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests may be sent to alvinj@cdatats.com. The legal contracting party and business address are identified in any signed proposal before paid work begins.
