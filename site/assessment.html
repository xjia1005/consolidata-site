Why these 30 questions?

Six starting questions establish the decision, customer situation, reported symptoms and available evidence. Six groups of four then examine customer reach, response, first useful result, purchase, continued use and evidence gaps. The report uses four customer-value questions: the desired result, confidence in achieving it, waiting time and customer effort.

Several explanations may remain possible. Your answers can support a useful next check; they do not independently establish a unique cause or guarantee renewed growth.
